Market Master is a web application that provides a comprehensive market analysis tool for trade ideas and insights. It offers a wide range of metrics to help users make informed decisions based on the latest market trends and insights.

  • Explore the screener following ISM manufacturing and services PMI Report
  • Check for top 15 manufacturing industries and services reporting growth and observe them in the screener by NAICS standard classification
  • Screener supports benchmarking by NAICS standard classification, sector (BICS level 1) and industry (BICS level 2)
